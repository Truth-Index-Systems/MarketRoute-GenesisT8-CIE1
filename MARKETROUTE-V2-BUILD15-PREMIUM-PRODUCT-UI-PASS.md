- plain-English commercial headings;

## Opportunity workspace hierarchy

The flagship workspace now answers, in order:

1. Is this company worth pursuing?
2. Why is it worth pursuing?
3. How strong is the research?
4. How do we reach the right person?
5. What still needs to be known?
6. What does the user need to decide next?
7. Is outreach in progress?
8. What evidence is the decision built on?

No commercial authority is calculated in the UI.
